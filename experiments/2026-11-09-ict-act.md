# OMX baseline ACT training, 2026-11-09

The baseline archive contains 50 robot demonstration episodes, 14,644 frames, 30 fps, two camera streams and six-dimensional action and observation vectors. Its task text is empty. The archive SHA-256 is `749f5208874cfd1e1f2e98539a39d962464f401023f3b423963246593bfaa250`.

A LeRobot 0.6.1 ACT run used 45 training and 5 held-out episodes, batch size 4, PyAV video decoding, and 1,000 steps on a Quadro RTX 5000. Held-out loss was evaluated on at most 200 samples: 0.6023 at step 200, 0.5552 at 400, 0.5363 at 600, 0.4503 at 800, and 0.3713 at 1,000. These are offline losses, not task success rates.

The final checkpoint was saved and read back on the ICT training machine; model weights are not included in this repository yet. The task objective, action units, timestamp alignment, episode quality and train-only normalization must be confirmed before further evaluation. No simulation or physical robot execution was performed for this model.

## Validation audit (2026-11-10)

The saved state and action normalization means match the full 50-episode dataset, including the five held-out episodes. Therefore the reported loss is not a fully independent held-out result and should be interpreted with that limitation. Separate train-only statistics have been prepared and their action/state mean and standard deviation checked against the raw 45 training episodes. A corrected training run and evaluation have not yet been performed.
